Curriculum vitae, updated 25 November 2026

Professional summary

Physician and biostatistician with expertise in clinical epidemiology, real-world data, and medical AI. Experienced in designing and executing hospital-based observational studies and regulated clinical research as a responsible biostatistician, with strong capability in translating clinical questions into reproducible analytical protocols. Actively leading Franco-Japanese collaborations in pharmacoepidemiology and health data science (including WHO VigiBase research and CANAL-AI), and experienced in securing research funding as a principal investigator for privacy-preserving medical AI and federated learning initiatives.

International collaboration and key projects

Franco-Japanese pharmacoepidemiology collaboration using WHO VigiBase. Sustained joint research with French partners; in 2025, contributed to seven VigiBase-related peer-reviewed papers, including three first-author papers.

CANAL-AI (Caen–Nagoya Alliance for AI in Health Data). International collaboration to develop privacy-preserving, federated workflows for hospital real-world data analyses in oncology-related research, with emphasis on cross-country harmonization, reproducible evaluation protocols, and operational feasibility under governance constraints.

Selected professional experience

Responsible Biostatistician, Nagoya University Hospital (2020–2025). Statistical responsibility for multiple regulated clinical research projects and investigator-initiated trials; extensive consultation and study design support across diverse clinical domains. Delivered tailored analytical strategies (multivariable modeling, survival analysis, mixed-effects modeling, meta-analysis, and machine-learning–assisted modeling) and supported interpretability-focused reporting for clinical stakeholders.

Biostatistics training, Nagoya University. Doctoral-level training in biostatistics and applied clinical research methods, with experience in statistical analysis for observational studies and development of robust, reproducible analytical workflows.
